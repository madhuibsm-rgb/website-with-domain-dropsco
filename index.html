We're building a long table. There's a seat for you.

Somewhere between 6:15 AM and 11:00 PM

You're good at your job in a way you don't say out loud very often.

You're the one who knows.

His cart is open and he's asking what else. You know what else — the curd, not the one on offer. The biscuits his mother actually eats.

You took the call at 7:40 from the car park. By eleven, three people were working on a decision you'd made.

You found out at nine last night about the solar system model. It got made.

At eleven you close the door. Ten minutes, the same book, the same tea. Nobody knows about this part, and that's rather the point.

Every one of them is you. Not one of them is all of you.

So we're setting
a Long table.

Not for a part of you. For the whole of you at once, which is a thing that almost never gets to sit down. Long, because it doesn’t stop at the first sitting. Someone brings a friend, someone leaves with one. Nobody here needs convincing. Nobody’s here to be fixed. Titles stay at the door — you know better than anyone that the more alive parts of you are somewhere else entirely.

Who's setting the table?

We're building a supplement brand for women. The same women we're building this table for.

Which is to say not a demographic to capture, not a statistic to sell into. A woman whose life keeps moving. Roles that shift by the decade and by the hour. A body doing its own shifting underneath all of it hormones, sleep, energy, the parts nobody prepared her for and very few people explain properly.

So we started here.
The product comes later.
